1. DEF press range (DEF v9 rule 8, /code/vpc/aws-aileague/prompts/def/v9.md). "An opponent has the ball in our half between the sides of our box and he is the opponent nearest our goal — PRESS him" lets a 900 ms DEF go as far as the halfway line. 2. In-box overlap (DEF rule 4 vs the shared Pressers line, affects DEF/MID/FWD1/FWD2). DEF rule 4 "An opponent has the ball in or at the edge of our box — PRESS him" fires for any in-box carrier, while the swarm's exclusion (MID rule 6, FWD1 rule 4, FWD2 rule 4) only exempts the carrier who is "the opponent nearest our goal". The unmarked attacker is pre-existing in v8 (same rule order, 0-2 shots on target against), the double press is new and harmless. Next version: extend the swarm exclusion to "a carrier in or at the edge of our box, or ..." in all five prompts, or make DEF rule 4 yield to rule 6 when an attacker is goal-side.
