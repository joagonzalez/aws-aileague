## Reviewer findings
- **Clarity: actions use platform command words**: "4. An opponent attacker runs behind you — Track the run and stay goal-side of them." — 'An opponent attacker runs behind you — MARK them, staying goal-side.'
- **Priority shadowing**: "4. An opponent attacker runs behind you — … (no possession condition)  sits above  6. You win the ball — Act at once. PASS forward …" — Rule 4's situation does not require the opponent to have the ball. If DEF wins the ball while an attacker runs behind, rule 4 fires first and DEF follows the runner while holding the ball instead of passing or clearing. Move 'You win the ball' up to rule 2, directly after the loose-ball rule. Alternatively, start rule 4 with 'The opponent has the ball and an attacker runs behind you'.
- **Contradictions: rule vs Personality**: "Rule 6: 'PASS forward to MID or a forward if they are unmarked.'  vs  Personality: 'Risk tolerance: low with the ball (no dribbling, no short passes near our box)'" — Split rule 6 by zone: 'You win the ball near our box — CLEAR it long toward FWD1 or FWD2. You win it further out — PASS forward to an unmarked MID or forward; otherwise CLEAR it long toward FWD1 or FWD2.' Drop 'Act at once.' to save characters, since the tempo line already covers it.
- **Contradictions: rule vs constraint**: "2. An opponent has the ball near our box — PRESS them at once. Stay between them and our goal and force them wide.  vs  NEVER leave the center in front of our goal empty." — When the carrier is wide near our box, pressing them takes DEF out of the center, and MID only drops when we are outnumbered. Decide which rule wins and write it down. Either add 'If they are wide, MOVE to block their path from the center and PRESS only when they come inside', or add to the constraint 'except to PRESS the ball carrier near our box'.

## Evaluator findings (team)
- **Pressing: exactly one presser per zone (their half)** (FWD1, FWD2, MID): In their half, FWD1 always presses but FWD2 also presses whenever it is closer, so there are two pressers. FWD1 rule 7 has no condition: "The opponent has the ball in their own half — PRESS the ball carrier hard." FWD2 rule 7 says: "If FWD1 is closer to the ball, INTERCEPT... If you are closer, PRESS the ball carrier." FWD2's Coordination repeats it: "you cut the passing lane unless you are closer to the ball." FWD1's prompt doesn't know FWD2 might press, and it keeps pressing too. This goes against MID's Coordination ("FWD1 presses in their half... Everyone else cuts passing lanes"), formation.md ("FWD2: Cuts passing lanes while FWD1 presses") and playbook Pattern 3 ("FWD1 in their half"). Two players chasing the ball is the failure match 001 showed: 124 presses, and the opponent still had 82% possession. Fix: FWD2 rule 7 becomes: "The opponent has the ball in their own half — INTERCEPT: cut the pass to the nearest free opponent. FWD1 presses." FWD2's Coordination becomes: "FWD1 presses in their half; you cut the nearest passing lane." Leave FWD1 as it is.
- **Pressing: exactly one presser per zone (zone boundaries) / Alignment with playbook** (MID, FWD1, FWD2, DEF): The pressing zones are defined differently in each prompt, so on one side of the halfway line two players press and on the other side nobody does. MID rule 6 says: "The opponent has the ball in midfield and you are our closest player to it — PRESS." FWD1 rule 7 presses anywhere in "their own half". (a) Ball in midfield on their side of the halfway line, MID closest: MID presses and FWD1 presses (and FWD2 too if it is closer than FWD1). (b) Ball in midfield on our side, with a forward holding near halfway closer than MID: MID rule 7 says "a teammate is closer to it than you — INTERCEPT", but FWD1 and FWD2 rule 8 say "Hold position near the halfway line" and DEF rule 5 says MARK. Nobody presses. The playbook says the presser is "chosen by zone", but MID and FWD2 choose by who is closest. Fix: Use the halfway line and DEF's own wording as the zone edges. MID rule 6 becomes: "The opponent has the ball in our half but not near our box — PRESS the ball carrier." MID rule 7 becomes: "The opponent has the ball in their half or near our box — INTERCEPT: stand in the passing lane to their nearest forward." MID's Coordination becomes: "FWD1 presses in their half, you press in our half outside the box area, DEF presses near our box." Change the MID line in formation.md ("presses in midfield when closest") to match.
- **Coordination is consistent both ways (GK distribution targets)** (GK, MID): GK counts on MID as a passing option, but MID's prompt never mentions receiving from GK. GK rule 4 says: "Second choice: MID if no opponent is near MID." GK's Coordination says: "MID is the short option only when free." MID's Coordination only says: "DEF passes forward to you when you are free." MID also has no rule for when GK or DEF has the ball in our half: rules 1–4 need MID to have the ball, 5–7 need the opponent to have it, and 8 needs us to have it in their half. So nothing makes MID offer itself for the pass GK expects. Fix: Change MID's Coordination line to: "GK and DEF pass forward to you only when no opponent is near you." The other option, which fits opponent-notes ("No short build-up from GK or DEF while they are pressing... Go long"), is to drop MID from GK rule 4 and GK's Coordination, so GK goes long to FWD1 or FWD2, or long toward the forwards anyway.
- **Coverage: no positional overlap** (GK, DEF): GK and DEF both go for the same loose ball in our box. GK rule 3 says: "The ball is loose inside our box and you are the closest player — Go and collect it." DEF rule 1 has no condition: "The ball is loose or crossed into our box — Get there first and CLEAR it." When GK is closest, both players run at the ball. DEF leaves its attacker free for the rebound, and neither prompt says who leaves it to whom. GK v1 did not have this overlap because it held the goal line until a shot came, so rule 3 introduced it. Fix: DEF rule 1 becomes: "The ball is loose or crossed into our box and you are closer to it than GK — Get there first and CLEAR it long and wide, away from goal." Add to DEF's Coordination: "When GK is closer to a loose ball in our box, leave it to GK and MARK the nearest attacker." Add to GK's Coordination: "DEF leaves loose balls in the box to you when you are closer."
