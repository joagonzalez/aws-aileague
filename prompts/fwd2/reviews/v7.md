Non-blocking notes, to watch next match or fix in the next version:

1. **Marking gap, by design.** Cutting passive MARKing removes v6 MID's second marker. With the opponent on the ball in our half, the cover is:
   - the presser on the carrier;
   - the pass to the opponent closest to the carrier cut;
   - DEF marking the attacker nearest our goal between the sides of our box, or any attacker inside it;
   - MID screening toward our goal when he is neither presser nor cutter;
   - GK on his line.

   A second runner outside our box who is not the carrier's closest option is left free. That is the brief's explicit trade-off, so I am not rejecting it. If we concede from a second unmarked attacker, MID rule 7 could MARK the attacker nearest our goal that DEF is not marking. Note that when MID is the presser, nobody screens between the ball and our goal except DEF.

2. **MID's long back pass to DEF (MID rule 2, and rule 4's second fallback).** Near their end line or a touchline, if neither forward is free, MID passes to DEF. That is a long ball the length of the pitch, which can be intercepted and leaves only DEF and GK. The forwards' fallback is MID, a short pass. Consider "the nearer forward" or "CLEAR toward the center of their half" for MID rule 2.

3. **Shooting trigger.** It is no longer identical across the three. MID drops the long-range clause (justified by match 007: 34 SHOOT commands, 0 on target). FWD1 and FWD2 add "no free teammate is nearer their goal with the goal in front of him", which costs an extra pass of about 1 s before a shot. The meaning is close to the brief's "straight in front and close". Watch for shots lost to that extra pass. MID's own changelog item (1) still says the trigger keeps the v6 meaning word for word; only the Reviewer-round note corrects it.

4. **Platform loose-ball quirk.** If DEF's ball at his feet at the box edge is reported as loose and "outside our box", MID rule 5 sends the nearest swarm player onto DEF's ball instead of to a free spot in front of him. Check: does MID crowd DEF on build-up?

5. **GK PASS.** If the platform only allows distribute from the hands, GK PASS will show 0 next match. The GK changelog already names the fallback ("distribute it to MID").

6. **"Never wider than the ball" is strict.** With the ball on the goal-to-goal line, the whole swarm lines up on it, so passes along the line can be cut by one opponent. Check whether "short pass apart" still finds free spots.

7. **MID rule 7 with their carrier in our box.** It puts MID a short pass toward our goal from the ball, which can mean inside our box near GK, in the shot path or GK's way. Minor.

8. **Strategy docs are out of date.** Formation.md and playbook.md still describe the Counter identity, zone pressing ("middle lane of our half — MID"), Pattern 4 with forwards at halfway, and the v6 Positioning Philosophy. Before deploy, update them for the 'Swarm' identity: shape, the shared Pressers line, pass-first build-up, and DEF clearing only under threat. Then the Alignment check holds and match logs can record Strategy: Swarm.
