# Playbook

## Tactical Patterns

### Pattern 1: Quick Counter
- **Trigger**: Win possession in our half
- **Execution**: DEF or GK plays long to MID → MID plays through-ball to FWD1 or FWD2
- **Key**: Speed. Don't hold the ball. Vertical passes over horizontal.
- **Who's involved**: GK/DEF → MID → FWDs

### Pattern 2: Controlled Possession
- **Trigger**: Win possession in midfield or higher
- **Execution**: MID holds, waits for FWDs to position, picks the better pass
- **Key**: Patience. Only pass forward when a FWD has separated from their marker.
- **Who's involved**: MID → FWD1/FWD2

### Pattern 3: Defensive Block
- **Trigger**: Opponent is attacking with numbers
- **Execution**: MID drops to form a pair with DEF. FWDs stay slightly higher to offer a counter-attack outlet.
- **Key**: Don't commit everyone back. Keep at least one FWD available for transition.
- **Who's involved**: DEF + MID (block), FWD1/FWD2 (outlets)

### Pattern 4: High Press
- **Trigger**: Opponent goalkeeper or defender has the ball
- **Execution**: FWDs press the ball carrier. MID cuts passing lanes. DEF pushes up to compress space.
- **Key**: Coordinated — if FWDs press, MID and DEF must push up too or gaps open.
- **Who's involved**: All outfield agents

## Default Approach

Start with **Pattern 2 (Controlled Possession)** as the base. Switch to:
- **Pattern 1** when we win the ball deep and opponents are out of shape
- **Pattern 3** when under sustained pressure
- **Pattern 4** selectively — high risk, high reward. Use sparingly.
